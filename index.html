CDC PLACES models 40 population health measures for every US county using survey data from the Behavioral Risk Factor Surveillance System (BRFSS). These are estimates, not exact counts — but they're the best available neighborhood-level health data, and they're completely public.

Data source: CDC PLACES 2025 release (BRFSS 2023). Model-based estimates have margins of error — individual values are shown with confidence intervals where available. This is not medical advice.

Data: CDC PLACES (public domain). Source code (MIT). No tracking, no ads, no signup.
